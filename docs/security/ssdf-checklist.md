## Summary (2026-10-31)

No SSDF practice is wholly Not met, and nothing here is a High finding. The gaps are four tasks: PO.2.2 (training), PO.5.2 (workstation baseline), RV.1.3 (vulnerability disclosure policy) and RV.3.2 (root-cause trend analysis). Partial practices also have unmet parts: signed build provenance (PS.3.2), tag protection and an independent release archive (PS.1, PS.3.1), the Ostium ASVS report (PW.4.4), and deployment defaults held by the go-live gate (PW.9). The disclosure policy must exist before the first non-synthetic user. Nothing in this checklist stops the Phase 0 exit: nothing is deployed, and no real personal data is processed.
