## Verdict

**BLOCK.** The design holds, and most of the implementation is careful work:

- The seccomp delta is additions only.
- The setuid and file-capability strip is correct and fails closed.
- The pull policy rejects remote pulls.
- The image allow-list parser fails closed.
- Every stop path removes the engine.
- The overlay leaves the #36 stack untouched when it's off.

It blocks on the contract G3 set for G6. It isn't blocked because I found an exploitable hole.

1. **G4-41-02 is not met on its capability clause (N41-01).**
   - The shipped `cap_add` is Docker's full 14-capability default set. The stop rule allows `SETUID`, `SETGID` "plus the recorded additions within Docker's default set". G2 says "one capability at a time", and G3 says `NET_RAW` and `MKNOD` are to be avoided unless an error is recorded.
   - No error is recorded for any of the 12 additions. The compose comment says they are "required for nested image unpack (chown/mknod)". G6's probes contradict that: **`SETUID, SETGID, SYS_CHROOT` runs the full Postgres workload identically to the shipped set** (the same `chown`, the same `mknod` refusal, `select 1` => `1`, port published).
   - The practical risk is low. The extra capabilities sit only in the bounding set of a uid-1000 process whose only file-capability binaries grant `cap_setuid`/`cap_setgid`. But this is exactly the "relaxation set past the tightened stop rule" case that G3 said would block. The fix is one line plus a re-run.
2. **Some "must" probes have no evidence (N41-10).** These are G4-41-12(d) (second half), (e) and (f), and the G4-41-04 and G4-41-08 demonstration runs. They fit into the same re-run.

Once both are resolved (or Marco records acceptance with a reason in the manifest), the verdict becomes **PASS-WITH-NOTES**. The remaining items are Medium or Low with a fix or a follow-up.
