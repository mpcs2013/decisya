**Title:** feat(api): Aspire AppHost + ServiceDefaults observability baseline (0.03)

Turns the stock Aspire template into the platform's observability baseline, and adds the `Decisya.Api` skeleton that serves the health call for the Done-when.

- **Requirements:** `docs/requirements/phase-0/apphost-servicedefaults.md` (G1, with the G5 traceability).

Changes:
- **`src/Decisya.Api`:** a skeleton with `AddServiceDefaults` and `/health` and `/alive`, mapped in Development only. It has no auth, business endpoints or database; #20 adds JWT validation.
- **ServiceDefaults telemetry:**
  - OTLP is the only exporter, and it is active only when `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_ENDPOINT` is set.
  - `Decisya.*` sources and meters are picked up automatically.
  - Health calls are now traced.
- **ServiceDefaults logging:**
  - Exactly two sinks: one-line JSON on stdout (`trace_id`, `span_id`, `tenant_id` and an HMAC-hashed `user_id`) and OTLP logs. OTLP scopes are forced off.
  - `[Sensitive]` masking fails closed on both sinks: members marked `[Sensitive]`, key names on a deny-list, JWT and `Bearer` strings, and the login, query and fragment of URLs, inside records, collections and framework types.
  - The `user_id` hash key is required outside Development. In Development a per-process key is generated.
- **AppHost:** wires `decisya-api`.
- **Tests:** three new test projects. 264 tests pass on the host, including a real AppHost run that correlates a `GET /alive` trace with its JSON log line. CI runs 263: the AppHost test (`Category=AppHost`) is filtered out, because it needs the Aspire runtime on the host.
- **`ci.yml`:** adds one `--filter-not-trait "Category=AppHost"` to the unit-test step.
- **Docs:**
  - `decisya.slnx` lists the new projects;
  - GETTING-STARTED step 2 now has the dashboard steps and a warning not to share the resource environment, which shows the OTLP key;
  - `nfr.md` gets NFR-10 to 12.
