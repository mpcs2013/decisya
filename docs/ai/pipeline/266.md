docs(adr): end-user authenticators at asvs level 3 (synced vs device-bound passkeys)

**Summary**

**ADR-0025 is accepted (Marco, 2026-11-03).** It decides authentication strength under ASVS 5.0 6.3.3 at Level 3. It is a decision only; implementation follows in later issues (F-1 to F-11, including #277).
- **End users:** WebAuthn passkeys with user verification, which may be synced; no password. 6.3.3's Level 3 clause (hardware-based, attested) is relaxed for end users under its own relaxation clause, with mitigating controls M-1 to M-13.
- **Admins and the Keycloak master-realm operator account:** two device-bound, attested keys each, and no TOTP, before the first non-synthetic tenant. `/api/admin` requires that level.
- **Recovery and every support case:**
  - a hold of at least 7 days, or 14 days for an account that held a device-bound key;
  - in-app and email notices, with a stop that goes to operator review;
  - restorable deletion;
  - email and name changes only at `device-bound` or through support, on every path;
  - re-enrolment at the original level;
  - no recovery codes.
- **Sensitive operations** need a device-bound proof at most 300 s old.
- **Accepted residual risks** (Marco, 2026-11-03): **R-CLOUD** (cloud-account compromise of a synced passkey) and **R-RECOVERY** (takeover by whoever controls the registered email, after the hold).
- **Conditional on Keycloak:** a negative result in the realm spike on attestation-chain verification, registration during step-up, or credential changes outside it re-opens decisions 1 and 9, and Greenlight is told.
- **In force:** the guarantees describe the target. Until the implementation lands, the ASVS baseline keeps V6.3.3 as Fail (C-01 at Level 3, C-02), and every user stays synthetic.
- **New NFRs:** NFR-63 to NFR-69.
- **Exchange:** D-016 (supersedes D-012, proposed for Greenlight's check and Marco's acceptance) and the G-008 reply are posted.
