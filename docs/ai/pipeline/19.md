**identity-dev** (`src/Decisya.Bff/**`, `tests/Decisya.Bff.Tests/**`), following G1 to G3:
- `Decisya.Bff.Proxy` holds a code-only YARP route `/api/{**catch-all}` to `https://decisya-api`, the header strip/add transforms, `Set-Cookie` removal, the upstream 5xx body replacement (a G3 addition), and the https check on the resolved destination outside Development (a G3 addition). It also holds the access-token, antiforgery and upgrade-rejection middlewares.
- `AccessTokenProvider` refreshes with a 30 s lead time behind a Redis lock (`RedisRefreshLock`). `invalid_grant` ends the session, after an S-3 re-read of the ticket; other failures return 503. `RedisTicketStore.TryUpdateTokensAsync` writes with `Condition.KeyExists`.
- Logout ends the Keycloak session server-side while holding the refresh lock (B-2, S-4). A failure there is ignored locally, logged and counted.
- The antiforgery check is shared by the `/bff` filter and the `/api` middleware (B-3, S-2). `Yarp` logs at Warning (S-1).
- G3 MUSTs G4-19-01 to 04: code complete at G4. G6 (F2 to F4) found three G3-named test cases missing: the logout-during-refresh race, the Keycloak 500 and invalid_client 503 cases, and three /api antiforgery cases. They were added after G6 and passed at once against the already-correct code, so they have no red run. The G4 claim "red, then green" was too broad (correction after G6). For G4-19-05, the server-side logout, its fail-open and the counters are tested; **the automated log-capture scan is not written yet** and goes to G5.
- Two traps found and fixed:
  - YARP reserves the policy name `default`, so the route uses `decisya-api-authenticated`.
  - `UseMiddleware<T>` resolves constructor dependencies at pipeline build, so `AccessTokenProvider` moved to an `InvokeAsync` parameter.
