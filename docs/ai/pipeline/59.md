**Scope addition (Marco, 2026-09-26): flaky test found by the new pre-push hook.**
- Marco's first `git push` of this branch was blocked by the pre-push check: `Decisya.Api.Tests` failed.
- Reproduced with 10 repeated runs of that project: run 3 failed in `TracingTests.A_call_to_alive_produces_a_completed_server_span` (line 48) with `InvalidOperationException: Collection was modified; enumeration operation may not execute.`
- Cause, a #15 test bug:
  - the server span ends on a server thread just after the response reaches the test client;
  - spans from parallel test hosts also land in the sink;
  - the assertion read the list without the processor's lock.
- A first fix (a locked snapshot only) failed 30 of 30 runs: the expected span had not ended yet when the snapshot was taken. That proved the timing race.
- The fix: `WaitForAsync` polls a locked copy until the expected span is present (bounded at 10 s), in both tracing tests.
