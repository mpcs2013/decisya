## G7 PR body draft

### Summary

Three new CI jobs run the real stack on every PR in the new `fullstack` lane (ADR-0019, Amendment 1). They follow the `image-scan` always-report pattern: `needs: changes`, no job-level `if`, `contents: read`, no secrets, no caches.

- **`apphost-tests`**: the `Category=AppHost` tests against the AppHost on the runner (#15).
- **`e2e`**: the AppHost (built DLL, ephemeral containers) plus Playwright, Firefox and Chromium with axe (#26). The dev password is generated per run and masked first (M1).
- **`zap`**: an unauthenticated ZAP baseline of the BFF origin, traditional spider plus seeded endpoints, no AJAX spider (Amendment 1). The verdict comes from our own `zap_policy.py`: Medium and High fail unless an expiring exception covers them, and any untrusted result fails closed.
- **Harness:** `.github/scripts/fullstack.py`. Secrets stay in child environments only. The AppHost console goes to a 0600 file. Results are scanned for secret needles (the password in four forms, JWTs, session and Keycloak cookies, the dashboard token, and PEM or base64-PEM private keys) before staging. ZAP's output sits inside a stop-commands window. On a readiness failure, it prints needle-filtered diagnostics.
- **Required checks (M2):** `main.json` and `FLOOR` gain the three jobs. Marco applies the live ruleset after they run green on `main` (`docs/runbooks/main-ruleset.md`).
- **Fixes found by the spikes:**
  - The OTLP-header AppHost test depended on the developer's user secrets; it now uses a per-run key.
  - #102's "no npx in ci.yml" guard is narrowed to `npx --no-install`, with `playwright install` allowed only in `e2e`.
  - Keycloak on the runner answers HTTPS on 8080.
- **Security finding F-123-S2:** spike 2's diagnostics printed the Aspire tunnel proxy's per-run TLS key into two public job logs.
  - Those logs were deleted, and the local copies too.
  - The proxy's logs are never fetched now, and new needles catch PEM, base64 PEM and `*_key` fields.

**Rollback:** revert this PR. If the live ruleset already lists `apphost-tests`, `e2e` and `zap`, remove them first (`docs/runbooks/main-ruleset.md`), or every PR waits for checks that no longer exist. No data, volume or secret to reset: everything runs on throwaway runners.
