## G7 PR body draft

Closes #333

**What**
ADR-0026 decides the administrative path from the application to the identity service (Keycloak), condition COND-02 of the Phase 1 plan. Option (b): Ostium supplies a narrow identity-operations provider in its Keycloak baseline. A service-account client holds one client role per operation and no `realm-management` role. Only a new Api module, `Modules.IdentityOps`, calls it, through audited, durable commands that carry the tenant. Each operation kind is capped per hour and per 24 h (enrolment tickets 5 and 10, email or name changes 5 and 10, destructive operations 30 and 60). Option (d), a manual console step, covers a closure's identity step during the invite-only beta only and ends before open sign-up. A dated ADR-0016 amendment adds one back-channel route from the Api's fixed address to the provider path; the published and BFF routes refuse it, and those refusals (D-3) merge before or with the provider mount (D-2). Ostium is asked for R-1 to R-5; Decisya's own follow-ups are D-1 to D-9 (about 17 to 25 working days). Documents only: no code, configuration or test changes.

Marco accepted ADR-0026 with the ADR-0016 amendment on 2026-11-20, and accepted the residuals R-APIOPS and R-PROVIDER by name at their full scope as stated in ADR-0026: R-PROVIDER is not bounded by the operation caps, and the beta date follows Ostium's R-1 release, because option (d) gives no re-enrolment.
