## G7 PR body draft

Closes #338

**What**
The SPA gets three public pages, the privacy notice (`/privacy`), the terms (`/terms`) and the contact page (`/contact`), readable without sign-in and linked from the footer of every view (epic EP-13). Each text exists in English, German and French, chosen by `?lang=en|de|fr` (strictly parsed; anything else is English and is never echoed), and every passage is a marked placeholder: a visible marker in its language, `data-decisya-placeholder` and the token `decisya-placeholder-not-final`, so the release-candidate gate (#339) can find each one. The terms state that the invite-only beta is for adults only; the contact page carries the complaint route, and the support address is one constant, the undeliverable placeholder `support@example.invalid`. The legal routes render before and without the session, so they stay readable when `/bff/me` fails or is rate-limited. The first device-storage allowlist (`src/Decisya.Web/src/legal/device-storage-allowlist.json`) lists the cookies sign-in and the session need; tests fail on any other cookie or storage key and on any request to a third-party origin. No change to the BFF, Api, realm or CI; no new package.

**Rollback:** revert the PR's commits. No data or configuration step: nothing is stored; the pages are static SPA content.
