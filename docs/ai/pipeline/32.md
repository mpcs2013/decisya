**G3's open question, answered on this machine's .NET 10 SDK, without the shape check:** `Guid.TryParseExact(…, "D")` accepts `0x…`, `0X…` and `+…` inside a component. For example, `0x5b1407-…` parses to the same GUID as `005b1407-…`. The new shape check (`HasCanonicalShape`) rejects these three forms (G4-32-01). The runtime itself already rejects the braced, parenthesised and bare-hex forms, wrong lengths, and fullwidth digits.
